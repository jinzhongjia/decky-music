关于这款插件About this plugin

Decky Music 把一整套音乐 App 装进了 SteamOS 游戏模式。它不是网页套壳：界面按 Steam Deck 的大屏与手柄重新设计，音乐服务、播放队列和音频输出跑在独立的 Rust 进程里——网络慢、解码忙、后端出错，都不会卡住你的 Steam。 Decky Music puts a complete music app inside SteamOS Game Mode. It isn’t a wrapped web page: the UI is designed for the Deck’s screen and controller, while music services, the play queue and audio output run in separate Rust processes — slow networks, busy decoders or backend errors never freeze Steam.

两大曲库，一个播放器Two libraries, one player

QQ 音乐与网易云音乐同时支持，在快捷菜单里一键切换。推荐、榜单、分类搜索、歌单 / 专辑 / 歌手详情、我喜欢与收藏歌单都在；正在播放页有同步歌词与翻译，网易云还支持逐字歌词和热评。 Both QQ Music and NetEase Cloud Music, switchable from the Quick Access menu. Recommendations, charts, categorised search, playlist / album / artist pages, liked songs and saved playlists — plus synced, translated lyrics, with word-by-word lyrics and hot comments on NetEase.

边打游戏边听，不打扰Keeps playing while you game

离开插件、进入游戏后继续连续播放。私人 FM、猜你喜欢、雷达推荐等电台无限续播；蓝牙耳机按键和系统媒体控件（MPRIS）可直接控制；播放出错时会弹 Steam 系统通知，玩游戏时也看得到。 Playback continues after you leave the plugin and launch a game. Endless radio (Personal FM, Guess-you-like, Radar), headset buttons and system media controls via MPRIS, and a Steam toast if something goes wrong — even mid-game.

稳，是第一需求Built to be boring-reliable

后端崩了会自动拉起并重注登录态；网络断了，下次按播放键从中断处接着放；上游超时先原地重试，不会悄悄跳歌。音质可选标准 / 高品质 / 无损三档上限，拿不到就逐档降级。 Crashed backends restart and re-apply your login. Lost the network? Press play and it resumes where it stopped. Upstream timeouts retry in place instead of silently skipping. Choose a Standard / High / Lossless quality cap with graceful fallback.
