系统需求System requirements

SteamOS

最低配置：Minimum:

  • 操作系统：OS: SteamOS 游戏模式（gamescope 会话）SteamOS Game Mode (gamescope session)
  • 处理器：Processor: x86_64
  • 运行库：Runtime: glibc 2.39+
  • 插件宿主：Host: Decky Loader（Steam 客户端 1784934043+ 需 v3.2.8-pre1+） (v3.2.8-pre1+ for Steam client 1784934043+)
  • 音频：Audio: 用户会话 PipeWire、ALSA 兼容层与 libasound.so.2PipeWire in the user session, ALSA compatibility and libasound.so.2
  • 网络：Network: 宽带互联网连接Broadband Internet connection
  • 存储空间：Storage: 两个后端二进制合计约 10 MB，另加插件本体About 10 MB for both backend binaries, plus the plugin itself

推荐配置：Recommended:

  • 设备：Device: Steam Deck（已完成真机验收）Steam Deck (verified on device)
  • 插件宿主：Host: 最新版 Decky LoaderLatest Decky Loader
  • 账号：Account: QQ 音乐需扫码登录；网易云部分歌曲可匿名播放，推荐与私人资产需登录QQ Music requires QR login; some NetEase tracks play anonymously, personal features need login
  • 音质：Quality: 对应平台会员可解锁高品质 / 无损A membership on the service unlocks High / Lossless
已知限制Known limitations
  • 歌曲是否可播、能拿到哪档音质，取决于账号权益、版权、地区与服务端状态；项目不提供代理或地区绕过能力。Playability and quality depend on account entitlements, licensing, region and service state; the project provides no proxy or region bypass.
  • 切换 QQ 音乐与网易云会停止播放并清空当前队列（两端歌曲 ID 不兼容）。Switching between QQ Music and NetEase stops playback and clears the queue (song IDs are incompatible).
  • 电台内容不跨会话保存；普通队列会恢复，但插件重启后不会自动开始播放。Radio isn’t persisted across sessions; the normal queue is restored but won’t auto-play after a restart.
  • 不做本地音频缓存，每次播放都重新拉流。No local audio cache — every play streams afresh.
  • 已有真机验收记录来自 Steam Deck；非 Deck 的 SteamOS 设备、ARM、桌面模式及其他发行版尚未验证。Device verification so far is on Steam Deck only; non-Deck SteamOS hardware, ARM, Desktop Mode and other distros are unverified.
