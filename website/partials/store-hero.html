Decky Music

社区中心Community Hub

在 SteamOS 游戏模式中享受 QQ 音乐与网易云音乐。手柄优先的大屏界面，扫码登录、推荐、搜索、我的音乐、电台与同步歌词一应俱全；边打游戏边听歌，后台连续播放，不拖慢 Steam。 QQ Music and NetEase Cloud Music, right inside SteamOS Game Mode. A controller-first big-screen player with QR login, recommendations, search, your library, radio and synced lyrics — keeps playing in the background while you game, without slowing Steam down.
