工作原理How it works

界面只跟 bridge 说话；bridge 是唯一的状态真相源；联网和出声交给插件沙盒外的两个 Rust 进程。 The UI only talks to the bridge; the bridge owns all state; networking and audio live in two Rust processes outside the plugin sandbox.

不拖垮 SteamNever takes Steam down

网络请求、解码与后端异常都在独立进程里，插件内降级为错误态；子进程崩了由 bridge 自动拉起。Network calls, decoding and backend faults stay in separate processes and degrade to an in-plugin error state; crashed children are restarted by the bridge.

不开放端口No open ports

bridge 与子进程走 Unix 域套接字 + NDJSON 协议 v1，不监听任何 TCP 端口；Rust 两端共用 wire crate。The bridge talks to its children over a Unix domain socket with NDJSON protocol v1 — no TCP listener at all. Both Rust sides share the wire crate.

不泄露凭证No leaked secrets

播放 URL、Cookie 与凭证从不写入日志或发给界面；二进制安装时按 SHA-256 校验。Play URLs, cookies and credentials never reach logs or the UI; binaries are SHA-256 verified at install.
